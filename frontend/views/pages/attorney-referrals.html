Attorney Referrals & Legal Partners

Connect with experienced immigration attorneys for legal representation and advice

Important: While we provide profile building and application preparation services, we strongly recommend working with a licensed immigration attorney for legal representation, advice, and petition filing. Below are resources to help you find qualified legal counsel.

Why Work with an Immigration Attorney?

Legal Representation

Attorneys can officially represent you before USCIS, handle legal complexities, and provide attorney-client privilege protection.

Legal Strategy & Advice

Receive expert legal opinions on your case, petition strategy, and how to handle complex legal issues or RFEs.

Risk Mitigation

Attorneys identify potential legal issues, ensure compliance with immigration law, and protect your interests throughout the process.

Peace of Mind

Having qualified legal counsel ensures your petition is properly filed and gives you confidence throughout the immigration process.

What to Look for in an Immigration Attorney

✅ Qualifications

  • Licensed to practice law in good standing
  • Immigration law specialization
  • AILA membership (preferred)
  • Experience with your visa category
  • Track record of successful cases

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Guarantees approval or specific outcomes
  • Not licensed or in bad standing
  • Requests payment to government officials
  • Advises you to lie on applications
  • Won't provide written fee agreement

Questions to Ask During Your Consultation

What is your experience with EB-1A/EB-2 NIW/O-1 cases?

Ask for specific numbers and success rates in your visa category.

Can you provide references or case examples?

Request testimonials or anonymized examples of similar cases they've handled.

What are your fees and payment structure?

Understand total costs, payment schedule, and what's included vs. additional charges.

How will you communicate with me throughout the process?

Clarify response times, communication methods, and who will handle your case.

What is your assessment of my case?

Get an honest evaluation of your chances and potential challenges.

Do you work with profile building services like ours?

Ensure they're comfortable with a collaborative approach if you're using our services.
