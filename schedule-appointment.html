Scheduling FAQs

How long is the consultation?

Initial consultations are 30 minutes. This is a focused session to assess your profile, discuss eligibility, and answer your key questions. Extended consultations can be scheduled if needed.

Is there a fee for the consultation?

Initial consultation is $299. This fee is credited toward our Complete Profile Building package if you decide to proceed.

What should I prepare?

Please have your CV/resume, list of publications, awards, and any other relevant achievements ready to discuss.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes! Please provide at least 24 hours notice to reschedule or cancel your appointment.

How will the consultation be conducted?

Consultations are conducted via video call (Zoom/Google Meet). You'll receive the meeting link in your confirmation email.
