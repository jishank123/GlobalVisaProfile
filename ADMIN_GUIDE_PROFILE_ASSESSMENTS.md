### Step 6: Take Action

**Quick Contact:**
- Click email → Opens your default email client with pre-filled address
- Click phone → Opens phone dialer (on mobile) or displays number (on desktop)

**Update Status:**
- Click "Update" button to change status
- Options: New → Contacted → In Progress → Completed

### Step 7: Prioritize Follow-ups

**High Priority** (Contact within 24 hours):
- ✅ Score 80%+: Excellent profiles, ready to apply
- ✅ Criteria met ≥3: Meets basic EB-1A requirements
- ✅ Status "New": Haven't been contacted yet

**Medium Priority** (Contact within 48 hours):
- Score 60-79%: Good profiles, minor gaps
- Criteria met 2-3: Close to qualifying
- In US location: Immediate need

**Lower Priority** (Contact within 1 week):
- Score <60%: Needs significant profile building
- Criteria met <2: Major gaps to address
- Long-term planning candidates

## 📊 Understanding the Data

### Overall Score Ranges
```
90-100%: Exceptional   → Recommend Premium Package, immediate application
80-89%:  Excellent     → Recommend Standard Package, ready to apply
70-79%:  Very Good     → Minor improvements, 3-6 month timeline
60-69%:  Good          → Some gaps, 6-12 month timeline
50-59%:  Moderate      → Profile building needed, 12-18 month timeline
40-49%:  Fair          → Significant work, 18-24 month timeline
<40%:    Developing    → Major profile building, 24+ month timeline
```

### Criteria Strength Levels
- **Strong (3 points)**: Meets USCIS standard, solid evidence
- **Moderate (2 points)**: Some evidence, needs strengthening
- **Weak (1 point)**: Minimal evidence, major improvement needed
- **None (0 points)**: No evidence in this area

### Criteria Met Interpretation
- **7-10/3**: Exceptional, apply immediately
- **5-6/3**: Very strong, minor enhancements
- **3-4/3**: Meets minimum, strengthen for safety
- **2/3**: Does not meet requirement, profile building needed
- **0-1/3**: Significant gap, long-term development required

## 💡 Best Practices

### Daily Routine
1. Open admin dashboard
2. Check "Profile Assessments" for new submissions
3. Sort by newest first
4. Review high-score assessments (80%+)
5. Contact within 24 hours

### Follow-up Strategy
```
Day 0:  New assessment submitted
Day 1:  Send "Thank you + Results" email
Day 2:  Follow-up call if no response
Day 7:  Send "Free consultation offer" email
Day 14: Final follow-up email
Day 30: Mark as "No Response" or "Not Interested"
```

## ✅ Quick Checklist for Each Assessment

When reviewing a new assessment:

- [ ] Note the overall score and strength
- [ ] Check if they meet minimum 3/10 criteria
- [ ] Review their field of expertise
- [ ] Note years of experience
- [ ] Identify their 2-3 strongest criteria
- [ ] Identify their 2-3 weakest criteria
- [ ] Determine realistic timeline
- [ ] Choose appropriate service package to recommend
- [ ] Send initial contact email within 24 hours
- [ ] Update status to "Contacted"
- [ ] Schedule consultation if interested
- [ ] Update status to "In Progress" once engaged
