🔧 Manual Testing Guide

  1. Open Client Profile: http://localhost:3000/client-profile
  2. Navigate to Services Tab: Click on the "Services" tab
  3. View Service Details: Click "Details" button on any service
  4. Select Payment Method: Click "Purchase" → Choose Online or Cash payment
  5. Test Online Payment: Fill out credit card form (demo mode)
  6. Test Cash Payment: Upload a screenshot and submit for verification
  7. Check Payment Status: Go to Payments tab to see status updates
