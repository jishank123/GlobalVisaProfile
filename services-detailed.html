Our Profile Building Services

Comprehensive, step-by-step support to strengthen your credentials and prepare a compelling immigration petition

Service Scope: We provide profile development consultation and application preparation assistance. We are not attorneys and do not provide legal advice or representation. For legal services, please consult an immigration attorney.

What We Do

Profile Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of your current credentials against visa requirements, identifying strengths and gaps.

  • Detailed criteria analysis
  • Evidence inventory review
  • Gap identification
  • Feasibility assessment

Strategic Roadmap

Personalized action plan with specific steps and timelines to strengthen your profile and meet visa criteria.

  • Custom development plan
  • Priority recommendations
  • Timeline milestones
  • Opportunity identification

Evidence Portfolio

Comprehensive organization and documentation of all evidence supporting your petition claims.

  • Document collection
  • Evidence organization
  • Portfolio compilation
  • Gap documentation

Detailed Service Breakdown

Phase 1: Initial Assessment & Strategy (Weeks 1-2)

Profile Evaluation

  • Review your CV, publications, awards, and achievements
  • Assess current evidence against 10 EB-1A criteria or relevant visa requirements
  • Identify which criteria you currently meet
  • Determine realistic criteria to develop

Gap Analysis

  • Identify missing evidence for each criterion
  • Evaluate strength of existing evidence
  • Prioritize development opportunities
  • Create timeline for profile development

Deliverables: Comprehensive assessment report, gap analysis document, and preliminary strategic roadmap

Phase 2: Active Profile Development (Months 1-12)

Awards & Recognition Development

  • Identify relevant award opportunities in your field
  • Prepare competitive nomination packages
  • Connect you with nominators and sponsors
  • Track application deadlines and requirements

Media Coverage & Press Strategy

  • Develop press-worthy angles for your work
  • Connect with relevant media outlets and journalists
  • Prepare press kits and interview materials
  • Track and document all media mentions

Judging & Peer Review Opportunities

  • Identify relevant conferences, journals, and competitions
  • Facilitate introductions to organizers
  • Prepare reviewer/judge applications
  • Document all reviewing activities

Professional Membership Guidance

  • Research selective professional associations
  • Evaluate membership requirements and exclusivity
  • Prepare application materials and nominations
  • Obtain documentation of outstanding achievement requirement

Original Contributions Documentation

  • Identify major contributions in your field
  • Gather evidence of impact and significance
  • Collect testimonials from field experts
  • Document adoption and implementation

Publication Strategy

  • Identify high-impact publication opportunities
  • Guide manuscript preparation and submission
  • Track citations and scholarly impact
  • Organize publication portfolio documentation

Phase 3: Application Preparation (Months 1-3)

Evidence Organization

  • Categorize all evidence by criterion
  • Create comprehensive evidence index
  • Prepare exhibit labels and tabs
  • Organize supporting documentation
  • Ensure all translations are certified

Recommendation Letters

  • Identify ideal letter writers (independent experts)
  • Provide letter writers with comprehensive briefing
  • Review drafts for completeness and strength
  • Ensure proper formatting and credentials
  • Coordinate submission and collection

Form Completion Guidance

  • Guide you through I-140 form sections
  • Help gather required biographical information
  • Assist with employment history documentation
  • Review completed forms for accuracy (non-legal)

Important: Final petition drafting, legal arguments, and official filing should be handled by a licensed immigration attorney. Find an attorney

Phase 4: Pre-Filing Review & Support (Weeks 1-2)

Final Checklist

  • Verify all evidence is included and organized
  • Confirm all translations are certified
  • Review recommendation letters for completeness
  • Ensure all exhibits are properly labeled
  • Double-check biographical information accuracy

Attorney Coordination

  • Transfer all organized materials to your attorney
  • Provide evidence summary and notes
  • Answer attorney questions about evidence
  • Support attorney's petition drafting process
  • Coordinate any final evidence needs
